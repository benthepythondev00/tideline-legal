PMS or PMDD?
How they differ

2 min read · checked against its sources

Most people who have periods notice something in the days before: a shorter temper, sore breasts, a low afternoon. That is premenstrual syndrome, or PMS. It is common and usually manageable.

Premenstrual dysphoric disorder, PMDD, is different in degree. The mood symptoms are strong enough to get in the way of work, relationships and ordinary days. People describe feeling like a different person for a week or two, and then like themselves again once their period starts.

The timing is the clue

What the two share is timing. Symptoms show up in the second half of the cycle, after ovulation, and ease within a few days of bleeding. If you feel the same all month, something else may be going on, and that is worth raising with a doctor too.
