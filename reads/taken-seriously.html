Being taken
seriously

3 min read · checked against its sources

Many people with severe premenstrual symptoms have been told it is just hormones, or stress, or part of being a woman. If that has happened to you, it says something about the appointment, not about you.

Bring the record

A dated record changes the conversation. It moves it from how you come across in ten minutes to what happened over two months. Hand it over at the start.

Say what it costs you

Doctors are trained to ask how much a symptom interferes with life. Be concrete. "I called in sick twice last month." "I nearly ended my relationship, and a week later I could not understand why."

Ask directly

It is fine to say: "I think this could be PMDD. Can we look at that?" If the answer is no, ask what else it could be and what the next step is. You can also ask to see someone else.
