Food and
cravings

3 min read · checked against its sources

Cravings before a period are real, and they are not a lack of willpower. Changes in appetite are one of the recognized premenstrual symptoms.

Eat little and often

Smaller meals every few hours can keep you steadier than two big ones. Having something ready makes it easier on tired days.

Turn a few things down

Less caffeine, salt and sugar in the two weeks before your period may ease some symptoms. You do not have to cut them out, just turn them down.

Foods with calcium

Calcium has been studied for premenstrual tiredness, cravings and low mood. Milk, yogurt, cheese and foods with added calcium are easy ways to get it. Talk to a doctor before taking supplements; food is the easy place to start.

Be kind about it

If you eat the chocolate, eat the chocolate. One week of cravings does not undo anything, and guilt only makes the week heavier.
