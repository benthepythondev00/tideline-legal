Getting ready for
the appointment

3 min read · checked against its sources

A short appointment goes further when you arrive with the facts ready. These are the things that help most.

Bring the record

Two cycles of daily notes are what the diagnosis rests on. Bring the report from Tideline, printed or on your phone, and offer it at the start rather than at the end.

Bring your history

Write down what you have already tried and how it went, especially antidepressants or the pill. If notes from another doctor matter, ask for them early. Records can take a while to arrive.

Know your first sentence

Start plainly: "I think this could be PMDD, and I have been tracking my symptoms." Then say what it costs you: days missed at work, fights you did not want, how the worst days feel.

Ask about other causes

No blood or hormone test can show PMDD. It is still reasonable to ask whether anything should be checked to rule out other causes, such as a thyroid problem.

If you feel dismissed, you can ask for a second opinion. Finding a doctor who knows PMDD is worth the effort.
