Questions for
your doctor

2 min read · checked against its sources

It is easy to forget your questions once you are in the room. Pick the ones that fit and write them down.

About the diagnosis

"Does my record fit PMDD, or something else?"

"Should anything be checked to rule out other causes?"

About the options

"What would you suggest trying first, and why?"

"How long before we know whether it is working?"

"What side effects should I look out for, and what do I do if I get them?"

About what comes next

"If this does not help, what is the next step?"

"Could I see someone who knows PMDD well?"

Keep tracking after the appointment. A few more months of notes show whether a change is making a difference.
