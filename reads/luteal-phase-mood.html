Tracking your mood
in the luteal phase

2 min read · checked against its sources · updated 10 Oct 2026

The luteal phase is the part of your cycle after ovulation and before your period, usually around two weeks. It is when many people notice a shorter fuse, low mood, poor sleep or cravings, and when PMS and PMDD symptoms show up.

What is worth writing down

How you felt, how strong it was, and how much it got in the way of your day. Sleep and energy help too. A word or a sentence is enough; what matters is that you write it on the day, not at the end of the week.

What it shows after a few cycles

One bad week could be anything. When the same feelings return in the same part of the cycle, and lift once your period starts, that is a pattern you can see and plan around, and one a doctor can read if you want to talk about it.

Knowing which phase you are in

A tracker should tell you each day where you are in your cycle, and show what you logged around the same day in earlier cycles. If your cycles are irregular, the estimate is rougher, and a good app says so.
