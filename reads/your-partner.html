Your partner and
the hard week

2 min read · checked against its sources

People who love you often take the hard week personally. From the outside it can look like you are angry with them, when what you are is overwhelmed.

Telling them in advance helps more than explaining afterwards. Pick a calm day. Say what happens, roughly when, and that it passes.

Be specific about what helps

"Be supportive" is hard to act on. "Handle dinner that week" is not. "Do not ask me what is wrong, just sit with me" is even better.
