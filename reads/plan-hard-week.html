Plan your
hard week

3 min read · checked against its sources

The hard week rarely arrives as a surprise. Tideline shows roughly when it starts, so you can plan for it while you still feel well.

Move what can move

Look at the week before your next period. Pick one thing that could happen a few days later instead: a deadline, a difficult conversation, a big social plan. One is enough.

Stock up now

Easy meals in the freezer, snacks that are not only sugar, the things you always run out of. Smaller meals through the day can help, and so can going easier on caffeine, salt and sugar in those weeks.

Protect your sleep

Plan early nights ahead of time. Too little sleep makes moodiness worse, and those are the nights that need it most.

Tell someone

Let the people close to you know the dates. "Next week is a hard one" is a whole sentence. It turns a sudden bad mood into something everyone saw coming.
