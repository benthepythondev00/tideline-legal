Morning-after pill
and your period

2 min read · checked against its sources

Emergency contraception works mainly by delaying ovulation. That is why it can change when your next period arrives.

After taking it, your period may come a few days early or a few days late, and it may be lighter or heavier than usual. For most people it arrives within about a week of when it was expected.

What Tideline does with it

If you log the morning-after pill, Tideline widens its estimate for that cycle. An app that carries on as if nothing happened will give you a confident date that is probably wrong.

When to check

If your period is more than a week late, or unusually light, take a pregnancy test. If you have severe pain low in your belly, see a doctor promptly.
