Sleep before
your period

2 min read · checked against its sources

Sleep often changes in the days before a period. Some people cannot fall asleep; others sleep far more than usual and still wake up tired.

Too little sleep can make moodiness worse, so those nights are worth protecting.

Small things that help

Go to bed and get up at about the same time, even on days off.

Put phones and tablets away for an hour before bed, and find a calm routine that tells your body the day is over.

Keep the bedroom quiet, dark and cool.

Swap late coffee or tea for a warm milky drink, and go easy on alcohol, which disturbs sleep.

When it lasts

If sleep problems carry on beyond the hard week, or leave you struggling to get through the day, talk to your doctor.
