For clinicians

Tideline is an iPhone diary for PMS and PMDD. Your patient writes one sentence a day; the app picks out the symptoms and how strong they were (none, mild, moderate, severe). After two recorded cycles she can save a two-page PDF to bring to you.

What the report shows

A day-by-day chart of total symptom strength with period days marked; the average rating of each symptom in the 7 days before each period next to days 4 to 10 of the cycle; how much it got in the way (her own 0 to 10 rating); and a few of her own sentences from the hardest days. It draws no conclusion and gives no score. It is a record of daily entries, not a diagnosis.

For your patients

Tracking is free, with no account; the entries stay on her phone and are never sent to us. The PDF report is part of Tideline Plus. Patients you send can open it free for a month with the code CLINIC: Report → Unlock → "Have a code?". After the month it renews at the monthly price unless she cancels in her iPhone settings.
