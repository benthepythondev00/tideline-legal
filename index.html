Tideline
Period & Mood

A private record of your cycle and how you feel, made for PMS and PMDD. Write one sentence a day. After two cycles you can hand your doctor a dated report instead of trying to remember.

What you log stays on your phone. There is no account and no server behind it.

Tideline is for iPhone.
