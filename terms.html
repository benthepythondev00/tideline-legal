The short version. Tideline is a personal record, not a medical device. Logging, your history and the export are free. Tideline Plus is a subscription you can cancel at any time in your iPhone's settings.

1. What Tideline is, and what it is not

Tideline helps you keep a dated record of your cycle, your mood and your symptoms, and turns that record into a summary you can show a doctor. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it does not replace a doctor. Only a clinician can tell you whether you have PMDD or anything else.

The cycle dates the app shows are estimates worked out from what you entered. They can be wrong. Tideline is not birth control and must not be used to avoid or plan a pregnancy.

The tips in the app are everyday ideas, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing any medication.

If you are thinking about harming yourself, do not wait for an app. Call your local emergency number or a crisis line now.

2. Your record

Your record is stored only on your phone (see the privacy policy). That means we cannot restore it for you. It moves to a new iPhone with your normal iPhone backup, and you can export it at any time for free.

3. Tideline Plus

Free: logging, your calendar and history, the export, reminders, the Apple Health import, two tips a day, some of the articles, and your partner seeing where you are in your cycle. Tideline Plus adds the tips chosen from what you log, what to expect each day, your hormone pattern, the doctor's report as a PDF, every article, and the heads-up for your partner with your notes on what helps.

  • Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. The price is shown in the app before you buy, in your currency.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. The subscription renews for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • If you choose the free month, you pay nothing today. When the month ends the yearly price is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. The app shows you the date before you agree, and reminds you two days ahead if notifications are allowed.
  • You manage and cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When a subscription ends, nothing you logged is locked or deleted. You keep your record and the free features.
